# Word data sources

- Vocabulary levels: *The CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.6*, compiled by Yukio Tono, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies. Downloaded from https://www.cefr-j.org/download.html on 2026-09-10. Permitted for research, education, and commercial use with attribution.
- Japanese glosses and frequency seed: EJDict-hand by kujirahand, https://github.com/kujirahand/EJDict, CC0 1.0 / Public Domain. Retrieved on 2026-09-10.

The shipped list is a derived selection for this learning app. It contains 1,800 A1/A2-centered review words and 500 additional A2/B1-centered entrance-exam practice words. It is not an official examination syllabus.
